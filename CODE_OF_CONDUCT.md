# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and precise, especially about P&L and risk
- Not presenting simulated results as live trading performance
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the research community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Investment advice presented as fact
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as API keys, without permission

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer via GitHub issues (or the email
listed on the GitHub profile). Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads,
or ban contributors.
